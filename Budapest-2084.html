Explaining the world

Our classic, physical world is said to be continuum in space and time. This world is played in 2continuum which means that you can create time-threads and you can play in these parallel worlds. The main goal of this game to present how a world like this could work.
Your other version in the alternate time-threads is called otherself. To create new threads, you need a non-deterministic random source, for example QRGs. You can distinguish the threads by IDs as well that can be seen on the status bar. With a device, called eigengram, you can communicate with your otherselves and also send keys to them. With a device, called chronogram, you can communicate with your pre-self, that is, your version from the past.
Although even this world has rules: no time-loops, no teleportation, only messages/keys can travel multiversal, objects or people cannot. After you died in a thread, just throw that thread away, and focus on the others you can continue. In some cases, you cannot avoid to sacrifice one of your otherselves' life to continue your way.

Story

We live in the post-war city, Budapest in 2084. We are just after the World War IV which was declared and escalated only in a few threads like ours. You're a Hungarian citizen of the city who tries to escape from this chaos. The technology does not serve the people but a regime who controls everything. If the secret police catch you in a single thread, they will catch in every threads. Gather devices that could help you to survive. Good luck!

Explaining the QRGs

The device QRG, that is, Quantum-Random Generator, is a non-deterministic randombit source. With its usage you can create time-threads. To be exact, in the 2continuum time-tree the time branches continuously, you just cannot notice it until you don't have these randombits that defines in which time-thread you just are.
You also can switch between the threads (click on the proper buttons in the status bar). And you also can throw a thread away. In different branches, you can behave differently.

Explaining the Eigengrams

It's possible that our real, physical world is 2continuum, too. We just cannot communicate between the threads. In this game you can. The device is called eigengram which branches the time and keeps a channel between them to use to send messages or a hardware key. It can be useful when one of your otherselves can get into a place for a key but cannot get out. Then the keys can be sent via eigengram to an otherself who could continue the game.
Watch out! The secret police can use eigengrams, too. If they locate you once, they located you in all the threads.

Explaining the Chronograms

The eigengram grants you not only multiversal communication but superluminal communication as well. If it can be combined with just a tiny time dilation, you could experience early phases in the messages. A device, that circulates a future message in an inside loop, can accumulate the early phases. It realizes the so-called chronogram. With this device you can branch a thread in the past and also send messages and keys.
Don't forget! You can communicate with your pre-self/post-self only until the channel is hold. So from the point you activated the device and to the point you sent the message. It is a great resource but has limitation in time. And it is important that it will not undo the steps you made in the past. It just branches the time in the time-tree to give you new chances in parallel.

People and animals



Police officer is a person with whom a meet means the halve of your money and taken quantum devices if had any.

Secret police officer is a person who kills you when you behave suspicious.

Dealer is a person who sells you quantum devices, or you can sell them as well. When you meet with them, press "P" for the deal ;)

Strangers are the simple people of the street.

Dead strangers are former strangers who died.

This is you in the game, yourself.

Your pre-self is just a shadow of your past. It is just a feature that helps you to orient in the space.

Your otherself is the other version of your character. You, as a player, can see them in one layer. In real life you could not see them.

A dog is an animal. Its behavior is similar to the strangers. Don't worry, it won't bite you. :)

Choose an object


The bomb is an object the explodes and destroy everything within 3 moves and 3 steps. It means you can just run away after discovering it. If you decide to try to defuse it, then stay at the bomb and press "D". After that you can choose to cut a wire of two. Choose wisely!

The car is a vehicle that moves. Watch out before stepping on the road, it can hit you.

The bus is similar to the cars but with different determinism.

The keys are hardware keys, so you cannot open doors directly. Get to a station which opens/closes doors with the key or does other things.

It's 2084. We have no traditional keys. To open or close a door you have to use a hardware key on a proper station.

At the stations you can control things if you have the proper keys, like opening/closing doors, initiating the self-destruction of the city, or ordering ice-cream.
