# Budapest 2084
A web game about the multiverse and time travel.

[![YouTube video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/8H1c8e8GrZk/0.jpg)](https://youtu.be/8H1c8e8GrZk)

## Introduction

This is a web game with minimalist graphics developed to demonstrate the concept of the time-tree (multiverse). Some design is inspired by the book 1984 (George Orwell) and the place is the fictive version of my, the author's city, Budapest in an alternate future, in 2084. The plot guides the player through levels. Each level means a logical puzzle you have to figure out. In this game the classical mindset is not enough, you have to use non-deterministic resources to go to the next level. It means branching the time-tree you can navigate your otherselves independently, with suitable devices (eigengram) communicate with them, including sharing keys to open doors or deactivate traps. With some devices (chronogram) you can also branch the time-tree in your past and communicate with your pre-selves. But watch out, the secret police observes you, and after you violate their laws in a possible time-thread, they are about to arrest you in all the other threads as well. This is 2084.

## How-to-run

## In-game Objects/Actors

Actors:
- Player: the goal is to get to the flag at least in a time-thread.
- Dogs: don't hurt you but can accidentally activate traps or spread infections.
- People in gray: they move random similarly to the dogs but with higher determinism.
- People in blue: police officers who get your items away.
- People in black: secret police officers who don't ask but shoot.
- People in gold: they offer you a deal (devices).

Vehicles:
- Blue car: moves predictably.
- Yellow bus: moves in highly nondeterministic pace.
- Red car: you can drive it.

Objects:
- Buildings, houses, fences.
- Bombs: some of them is timed and you can just escape from the explosion, some is not, but you always can attempt to deactivate it (cutting the correct wire).
- Doors: you can cross some of them, in some case, only after opening them at the PC stations with hardware keys.
- PC stations: with the right keys, you can deactivate traps, open doors and so on, combining with eigengram, you also can upload your hardware keys to your otherselves.

Items:
- Money: banknotes on the ground.
- QRGs: quantum random generators, its usage means a non-deterministic random event which branches your time-thread.
- Eigengrams: after activation, branch the time-tree, then you can communicate with your otherselves cross the threads.
- Chronograms: after activation, you can decide to send a message back the time when you activated this device and it also means a branch from that point.
- Keys: hardware keys with you can deactivate traps, open doors and so on, you also can upload to your otherselves via an eigengram at a PC station.

## Keyboard control

### Webapp

Arrows to move right, left, up or down.

Space to skip a move.

Key 'D' to try to defuse an activated bomb (then click on the right button to cut one of the wires)

### Mobile app

Swiping the field into horizontal/vertical direction to move left, right, up or down.

Touch your player at a bomb: try to defuse the bomb.

Touch your player at a PC station: try to activate the station with your keys.

Etc.
